📖 LessonCraft — User Guide

Welcome to LessonCraft

LessonCraft helps Ontario educators create structured, curriculum-aligned lesson plans and export them as professionally formatted Word documents. Everything runs in your browser — no account, no login, nothing sent to a server.

💡 Your work auto-saves to your browser. Close the tab and come back — it will still be there.

The Sections

1 — Lesson Information

Teacher name, date, duration, grade, strand, and lesson title. These appear in the document header on export.

2 — Curriculum Connections

Overall Expectations (broad curriculum goals) and Specific Expectations (measurable, lesson-level outcomes). Use + Add for multiple entries.

3 — Learning Goals & Success Criteria

"We are learning to…" goals and "I can…" success criteria. Aim for 2–4 of each.

4 — Achievement Chart

How this lesson addresses Knowledge & Understanding, Thinking, Communication, and Application.

5 — Assessment

Diagnostic (before), Formative/For Learning (during), As Learning (self-assessment), Summative/Of Learning (end product), plus strategies and tools.

6 — Considerations for Planning

Prior learning, IEP/ELL differentiation, technology, and materials needed.

7 — Three-Part Lesson

  • Minds On — hook activity, activates prior knowledge (~10–15%)
  • Action — working activities (~60%). Add as many as needed with + Add Activity.
  • Consolidation — bring learning together (~10–20%)

8 — Learning Skills

Check relevant skills and add observable "I can" criteria. Focus on 2–3 maximum.

Saving & Loading

💾 Save

Downloads your plan as a .json file for backup or moving between devices.

⋯ → 📂 Import

Load a draft from a file, paste JSON from an AI, or start from a Template.

💡 Importing overwrites the current form. Save first if needed.

✏️ Edit Layout

Open ⋯ → Edit Layout to customise the form structure:

  • ↑ ↓ — reorder sections
  • Click title — rename a section
  • 👁 / ○ — show or hide a section
  • + Add Section — add a custom blank section
  • Save as Template — save your layout for reuse
💡 Click ✓ Apply Layout to save changes and re-render the form.

🎨 Theme Editor

Open ⋯ → Theme Editor to personalise the look:

  • Presets — 6 ready-made colour schemes (Classic Blue, Forest Green, Slate, Burgundy, Midnight, Minimal)
  • Colors — customise primary, secondary, highlight, background, and body text individually
  • Fonts — choose heading and body typefaces independently

Your theme applies to both the app and the exported DOCX. It travels with your .json draft — import on another device and the theme is restored.

💡 Click ✓ Apply to save. Close without applying reverts to the last saved theme.

📋 Templates

Open ⋯ → 📂 Import → Templates tab. Built-in templates load instantly. Click ☁ Load Online Templates to fetch subject-specific starters from the template library.

Save your own templates from the Edit Layout screen — they include your layout, section titles, and current form data.

📖 LessonCraft — User Guide

Welcome

The LessonCraft helps Ontario educators create structured, curriculum-aligned lesson plans and export them as professionally formatted Word documents. Everything runs in your browser — no account, no login, nothing sent to a server.

💡 Your work saves automatically as you type. Close the tab and come back — it will still be there.

Getting Started

1
Fill in the 8 sections from top to bottom. Fields marked * are required.
2
Track your progress with the gold bar at the top and the Plan Completion strip.
3
Export when ready — click ⬇ Export for a professionally formatted Word document.

The 8 Sections

1 — Lesson Information

Teacher name, date, duration, grade and subject, curriculum strand, and lesson title. These appear in the document header on export.

2 — Curriculum Connections

Overall Expectations (broad, coded curriculum goals) and Specific Expectations (measurable, lesson-level outcomes). Use + Add to include multiple expectations.

3 — Learning Goals & Success Criteria

"We are learning to…" goals and "I can…" success criteria students use to self-assess. Aim for 2–4 of each.

4 — Achievement Chart

How this lesson addresses the four Ontario categories: Knowledge & Understanding, Thinking, Communication, and Application.

5 — Assessment

Diagnostic (before), Formative/For Learning (during), As Learning (self-assessment), Summative/Of Learning (end product), plus instructional strategies and assessment tools.

6 — Considerations for Planning

Prior learning, IEP/ELL differentiation, technology used, and materials needed.

7 — Three-Part Lesson

  • Minds On — hook activity, activates prior knowledge (~10–15%)
  • Action — main working activities (~60%). Add as many as needed.
  • Consolidation — bring learning together, check understanding (~10–20%)

8 — Learning Skills

Check relevant skills and add "I can" criteria. Focus on 2–3 maximum.
